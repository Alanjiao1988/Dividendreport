Volume elasticity is *estimated* at ~27% incremental margin (slot, terminal-handling and bunker costs scale with volume). Capacity figures are BIMCO/Alphaliner *estimates*.

**Limitations — important.** These are **local** sensitivities around the Base case and are deliberately sized small (±USD 50/TEU) because container shipping is violently non-linear: near the cost curve an incremental USD 100/TEU swings profit by roughly **RMB 15bn**, i.e. half of normalized earnings, while further above it margins compress as carriers add capacity. Drivers are also **correlated** — a rate collapse normally arrives with soft volume, cost deflation *and* lower interest rates — so summing single-driver results is invalid, and the isolated rows **understate** the true downside in a genuine cycle turn. The FY2027 Bear case in Section 11 (NP RMB 12bn) is the correlated view and should be read as the real stress test.
